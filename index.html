We fly customised FPV drones, piloted through goggles from the drone's point of view. That puts the camera beside a drift car mid-corner, or over the edge of Bluff Knoll.

We film circuit racing and drift, rally, motocross, mountain biking, jet sprint, off-road, coast and ranges, and burnout competitions.

Services

  • Event films

    We film the whole meeting and cut a highlight film for the organiser, plus short clips for social.

  • Live broadcast

    A multi-camera stream to YouTube with drone and trackside cameras and live timing on screen. The replay stays up afterwards.

  • Brand and sponsor content

    Footage built around your product or your sponsor. Send us a shot list and we'll plan the flights to match.

  • Destination films

    Low-level flights over coast and country. We've flown Steep Point, Bluff Knoll and Windy Harbour, and overseas at Mount Bromo and in the Dolomites.

  • Season coverage

    The same crew at every round, so your series looks consistent from the first round to the last.

  • Competitor clips

    Your own run, filmed from the drone. Book before the event and we'll send your clip after it.

Events we've filmed

The D1WA drift series, the Australian Historic Road Racing Championship, Targa West, West Coast Jet Sprint, the Brookton Pony Express and Bindoon Burnouts.
