Capabilities

  • Event coverage

    Race meetings, festivals and shows, filmed from start to finish. The organiser gets a highlight film and short clips for social, and competitors can book a clip of their own run. For a series, the same crew covers every round.

  • Live streaming

    A multi-camera stream to YouTube with drone and trackside cameras and live timing on screen. The replay stays up afterwards.

  • Real estate

    Aerial and FPV video of homes, land and developments, for a listing or a sales campaign.

  • On-the-ground videography

    Camera operators on the ground as well as in the air, so a film or a broadcast can cut between trackside and drone footage.

  • Brand and sponsor content

    Footage built around your product or your sponsor. Send us a shot list and we'll plan the flights to match.

  • Nature and destinations

    Low-level flights over coast and country. We've flown Steep Point, Bluff Knoll and Windy Harbour, and overseas at Mount Bromo and in the Dolomites.
